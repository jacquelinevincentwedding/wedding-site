Travel

There are two airports that serve Miami: Miami International Airport (MIA) and Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL). We recommend flying into MIA, which is closer and more convenient.

  • Recommended

    Miami International Airport (MIA)

    About 8 miles from Vizcaya and our recommended hotels in Coconut Grove.

    Roughly a 20-minute drive.

  • Fort Lauderdale–Hollywood International Airport (FLL)

    About 30 miles north of Coconut Grove.

    Usually a 45-minute to one-hour drive, and longer in traffic.
